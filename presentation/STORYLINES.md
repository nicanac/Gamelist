# Storylines

Three different films about the same subject: the Game Library app and the collection it shows. Every
number below comes from the Notion snapshot in `src/data/library.json` (237 games, synced
Oct 27, 2026).

**Built: A**, because a stranger follows one game they know (Breath of the Wild) from a plain row
of data to a library that moves, and it covers both halves of the brief: how Notion feeds the app
and what the collection holds.

## A. One long list (built)

- **Device and form:** life before and after, carried by a number that lands; an object-to-object
  chain (the list folds into its count, the count is the app's own curtain, one search finds one
  row, that row's cover pulls back into all 237).
- **What the viewer sees and feels:** the real rows of the Notion database scroll past in a plain
  table until they fold into the number 237. That number is the app's own count-up, which lifts
  into the moving library. A search for "zelda" finds Breath of the Wild, its detail view fills in
  from its row, and its cover pulls back into all 237 covers sorting themselves by status.
- **First two seconds:** frame 0 is the table, full bleed, on paper: the column names `name`,
  `platforms`, `status`, `mc_score`, `hltb_main_story` and real rows already scrolling up. "237
  games in Notion" arrives word by word above it by 0.6 s.
- **Beats:** hook (the rows) → tension ("One very long list", the scroll speeds up) → reveal (the
  rows fold into 237, which cuts to the app's curtain at 237 and lifts into the hero) → proof (type
  "zelda", 41 cards rearrange, open Breath of the Wild, its row's values land on the facts) →
  payoff (the cover pulls back into all 237 covers, which sort into Played 78, Playing 23, Up next
  111, Dropped 25) → claim ("Same Notion list. Now it moves.") → end card (the mark fills, the
  wordmark wipes in, the repository).
- **Look:** two worlds that share one type system. The table: paper `#f2f1ee`, ink `#08080c`,
  Geist Mono values. The app: its own night `#08080c` with the violet and pink aurora, the holo
  gradient and Unbounded Black capitals, as captured.
- **Sound:** no music source in this environment, so no bed. Effects only on visible actions: a
  whoosh on the fold and on each world change, the real click and key ticks of the search, one pop
  when 97 lands, one riser into the sort.

## B. 237

- **Device and form:** a number lands; one frame that rebuilds.
- **What the viewer sees and feels:** the app's count-up reaches 237, and the frame keeps
  rebuilding around that number: 78 finished, 23 playing, 111 up next; 19 platforms led by PS4
  (71) and Switch (49); Zelda (39) and Mario (32) as the biggest franchises; release years from 1986
  to 2025; about 926 hours of known backlog. It ends on the hero.
- **First two seconds:** 237 already fills 80% of the frame width, and the cover wall floods in
  behind each digit.
- **Beats:** hook (237) → status split → platforms → franchises → years → backlog hours → the hero
  as the claim → end card.
- **Look:** the app's night only, every number in Unbounded with tabular figures, the status
  colours as full-bleed fields.
- **Sound:** a tick under each count, a pop on each landing.

## C. Play it back (the bold one)

- **Device and form:** the result first; one continuous take, in reverse.
- **What the viewer sees and feels:** inside the detail view of a game being played (Super Mario
  Odyssey, Metacritic 97), the camera pulls back without a cut: through its card, the filtered
  grid, the stats, the hero, out of the page, until the whole site is one row in a Notion table.
  Then it dives back in.
- **First two seconds:** the 97 ring filling, full frame, the game's poster behind it.
- **Beats:** the result → the grid → the numbers → the hero → the row in Notion → dive back in
  → end card.
- **Look:** one continuous camera over captured footage, ending in the paper table world.
- **Sound:** one long pulled-back whoosh, then the tick of rows at the far end.
